### Full description (max 4000)

<!-- full:en -->
MediQ helps clinics in Pakistan run a calm waiting room. Reception gives tokens, doctors see their own line, and patients know when their turn is near, without crowding at the desk.

FOR DOCTORS
• Today's queue at a glance: waiting, done and missed patients
• Call the next patient in one tap
• Notes, diagnosis and prescription with every visit
• Your schedule for the next two weeks, working days and leave
• Set your hours and breaks; mark when you are running late

FOR RECEPTION
• Walk-in tokens in seconds, one line per doctor
• Now serving, call again, skip and no-show
• Late and break notices that patients see on their token page and the TV

FOR CLINIC OWNERS
• Today's numbers for your clinic on your phone
• Invite doctors and receptionists to your clinic

FOR PATIENTS
• Scan the clinic's QR code to join the line
• Follow your place in line live, and get an alert as your turn comes

Works in Urdu and English on the web. The waiting-room TV display shows the line in both languages.

MediQ is a queue and records tool for clinics. It does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call 1122.
